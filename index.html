RideGlance
Battery, range and ride insights at a glance.

RideGlance is a free, ad-free Android companion app for TENWAYS CGO800 Plus riders. See live bike data, record rides, understand battery efficiency and build personalised range estimates from the way you actually ride.

What RideGlance gives you

A focused view of the information that matters while keeping ride data on your device.

Live ride data

Battery, range, speed, power, trip stats and lights.

Personalised range

Range estimates based on your recorded rides and observed battery use.

Battery efficiency

See percentage used per mile, miles per 1% and estimated Wh per mile.

Ride history

Review saved rides, battery use and distance over time.

Private by design

RideGlance has no ads, analytics, user accounts or cloud service. Ride and diagnostic data stay on your device unless you deliberately choose to share them.
