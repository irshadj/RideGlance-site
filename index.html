RideGlance
Battery, range and ride insights at a glance.

RideGlance is a free, ad-free Android companion app designed exclusively for TENWAYS CGO800S Plus riders. See live bike data, record rides, understand battery efficiency and build personalised range estimates from the way you actually ride.

Compatibility: TENWAYS CGO800S Plus only. Other TENWAYS models are not currently supported.
